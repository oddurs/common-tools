## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Any number of glyphs, each its own menu bar item showing an image, an animated GIF, an emoji, or a piece of text.
- A Settings window with a sidebar to add, duplicate, hide, and delete glyphs, and an editor for each: light and dark menu bar previews, a crop editor, rotate and flip, three styles (**Colour**, **Silhouette**, **Ink**), background removal by edge colour or by Vision's subject lift, and size. **General** holds **Open at login**.
- A menu on each glyph to edit it, choose or paste an image, add a glyph, or hide this one, and dropping an image onto a glyph to replace its image.
- New glyphs start beside the clock, so a menu bar manager doesn't hide them.
