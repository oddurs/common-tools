## Problem

The state must be readable at a glance, and the common choices one click away.

## Proposal

A click toggles indefinite; the menu offers the timed and conditional rules from the milestone; the icon fills while active and its tooltip says the rule and time left.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every rule can be started and stopped from the menu.
- [ ] VoiceOver reads the state and the time left.
